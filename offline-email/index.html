Before you start

Install HushboltAI from the App Store while you’re online. The initial download is about 1.9 GB because the models are included. Once it’s installed, you don’t need an account or another model download to write.

Try it in Airplane Mode

  1. Disconnect. In Settings, turn on Airplane Mode and check that Wi-Fi is off too. Wi-Fi can stay on if you’ve switched it on during a previous flight.
  2. Open a new conversation. Open HushboltAI and type the sample request below. The example uses the keyboard, so no microphone permission is needed.
  3. Compare the wording. Does it still ask the same person for edits, and are they still due on Friday? If a detail changed, edit the result before copying it to your mail app. Reconnect when you’re ready to send.

Where does the text go?

The model runs on your device. If you want this conversation to stay on this device, leave private iCloud conversation sync off in Settings & privacy. Turning sync on can copy saved chats to your private iCloud when you’re online. It doesn’t change where replies are generated.
