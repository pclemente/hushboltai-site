# HushboltAI: private AI tools for iPhone and iPad

[Download free on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6837171118) · [Explore the app](https://pclemente.github.io/hushboltai-site/) · [En español](https://pclemente.github.io/hushboltai-site/es/)

Turn a rough email into a kinder request, or turn your notes into a checklist. HushboltAI generates replies on your own device using the models included in the app. Once installed, you can type a request in Airplane Mode without signing in or downloading a model.

[Try an offline email rewrite](https://pclemente.github.io/hushboltai-site/offline-email/): start with “Send me your edits by Friday,” compare it with a real model reply, and check that the deadline is still there before you send it.

[How fast was it on an M1 iPad Pro?](https://pclemente.github.io/hushboltai-site/m1-ipad-ai-speed/) In one cold-start test, a 359-word story was ready to read in 30.05 seconds.

Each device gets 10 free new completed replies a day. Optional Plus is a one-time purchase for unlimited replies from the included models and Markdown export in this edition. Chats stay on your device unless you enable private iCloud sync.
