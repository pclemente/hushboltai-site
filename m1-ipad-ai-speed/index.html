On-device speed test

How fast is offline AI on an M1 iPad Pro?

On an 11-inch M1 iPad Pro, HushboltAI generated a 359-word story entirely on the iPad. Starting with the model unloaded, it took 30.05 seconds for the reviewed story to become available to read.

What we tested

On November 24, 2026, we started a fresh conversation in the normal reply-length mode on a third-generation 11-inch iPad Pro with M1. HushboltAI selected the included Qwen3.5 2B model. It finished the story without a retry or a manual continuation.

We used a separate test reader with the same on-device engine and model files as HushboltAI 1.1.1. This was one run, not an average or a timing of the full App Store chat interface. We didn’t measure warm starts or peak memory. A different device or request can give a different result.
